Everything below was deliberately removed. Nothing was altered: each kept file
is row-for-row identical to its counterpart in the full releases.

## Reference works dropped (9,116)

Only the 25 lexical works survive. Removed: 162 commentaries, 7,428 Rabbinic
and Targum works, 674 systematic-theology volumes, 302 mysticism, 179
hermeneutics, 140 Apocrypha, 97 liturgy, 86 patristics, 55 sermons, 54
Reformation, 46 church history, 11 morphology, 8 cross-reference systems,
9 topical works, and 3 general English dictionaries (Webster 1806, 1828,
1913) that are not biblical lexicography.

Deliberately not included, having been weighed and excluded: the Klingon
lexicons, Spurgeon's *Morning and Evening* (a devotional, filed under
Lexicon in the source catalogue), and the Swedish glossary — see the
release notes for the per-item reasoning.

The complete list of all 9,141 works is in the parent releases
(`reference-sqlite-rev5`, `bible-reference-v5`).
